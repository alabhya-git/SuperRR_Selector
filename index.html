Does the definition find consequential balls?

In the 2026 check, the top pressure band contained more win-probability movement than its share of balls. Closeness alone also finds consequential moments; this is evidence for relevance, not proof of unique superiority.

A crucial distinction

A high PRR can reflect a strong overall level even when a player's raw pressure-minus-usual change is negative. The evidence card shows both, so the rating never masquerades as an observed “clutch” effect.

05 / THE METHOD

How to read the answer.

01

Define the moment

Estimate pre-ball match leverage, then separate high-pressure balls from a player's usual situations without using the outcome of that ball to define pressure.

02

Value the contribution

Translate batting and bowling events to runs above expectation in their situation. Compare players within role and control noisy small samples.

03

Reward reliability

Combine level, situation and consistency with shrinkage. Report sample size, uncertainty, repeat appearances and sensitivity to alternate choices.

04

Keep the auction separate

Freeze merit ranks first. Layer official retention status, RR's role needs, availability and price onto the shortlist only after the performance analysis.

Read this before using a rank

The supplied data spans multiple competitions, so role, league context and sample size matter. Raw pressure changes can disagree with the modelled rating. Split-half “clutch” evidence is weak, and predictive backtests are mixed. No single number should be treated as a guaranteed 2027 outcome.

This public dashboard contains summary aggregates only. It does not expose the competition data pack or ball-by-ball records.
